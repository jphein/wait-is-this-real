## Notes
- Several official sites (ssa.gov, dhcs.ca.gov, fcc.gov, californialifeline.com) block curl and WebFetch with 403 or an Incapsula JS challenge but load in a real browser. A scam-checker that health-checks its URLs needs a browser user agent, or it should treat 403 as "up".
- Not verified: hours of operation beyond what the source pages state, and whether the PG&E Spanish line is 1-800-660-6789 or 1-877-660-6789. On the contact page the visible text says 1-800, but the tel: link dials 1-877.
